# Privacy boundary

The public control plane contains no personal facts. The private vault may contain reviewed personal notes, while identity records, credentials and raw medical material require stronger isolation or encryption. Privacy scanning is advisory: unsupported binary files always require manual review.

All unknown extensions, links/reparse points, invalid text and read/enumeration
failures are reported as unreviewed or incomplete. `doctor -Quick` does not run a
privacy scan; HEALTHY is configuration health, never a privacy certification.
Reports retain paths that can themselves contain private titles. Do not upload
reports or logs without review. The five built-in pattern categories do not
recognize all contact/address/medical/secret content; human review remains needed.
